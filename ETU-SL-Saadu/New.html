The BADEA Project Monitoring Team and the Administration of ETU-SL Visit Bunumbu Campus

28 August 2026

The BADEA Project Monitoring Team, led by the Project Coordinator (PC), together with the Administration of the Eastern Technical University of Sierra Leone (ETU-SL), led by the Vice Chancellor and Principal (VC&P), and stakeholders of Bunumbu Town, visited Bunumbu Campus on Thursday, 27 August 2026. The visit assessed ongoing reconstruction and rehabilitation works funded through the Government of Sierra Leone under the BADEA Project.

Tour of the Facilities

During the tour, the team inspected several facilities and noted significant improvements in many areas, as outlined below:

  • Primary School: Reroofing, tiling and ceiling works completed; electrical work and ramp pending.
  • Clinic: Painting, roofing and tiling completed; electrical work pending.
  • Staff Quarters: Approximately 90% complete, with electrical work outstanding; contractor’s quarters not yet rehabilitated.
  • Library: Ceiling work still pending.
  • Administrative Building: One-storey block prepared for roofing; preparation for concreting the last floor of the three-storey block in progress.
  • Female Hostels: Roofed with windows fixed; tiling and ceiling works yet to commence.
  • Student Canteen: Approximately 95% roofed; tiling and ceiling works pending.
  • Lecture Halls: Roofed, with tiling in progress.
  • Multipurpose Hall, Chapel, Geography Building, Pavilion, Male Hostels and Staff Canteen: Minimal or no progress since the last visit.
  • Rural Technology Building: Fully roofed, with tiling ongoing.
  • Filling Station: Significant work in progress.
  • Guesthouse: Tiles fixed; doors and windows pending.

Debriefing Session

Following the tour, the monitoring team convened a debriefing session to record significant observations regarding the implementation of the project.

The beneficiary institution, ETU-SL, expressed appreciation to the contractor for the level of work accomplished thus far and urged speedy completion to enable the final handover of the facilities.

The Monitoring and Evaluation Officer from the Ministry of Finance encouraged the contractor to intensify efforts, stressing the importance of completing the project before the closure of the BADEA Support Window.

The Project Coordinator joined the previous speakers in commending the contractor’s progress but emphasised that much work remains to be done. She reminded the contractor that December had been communicated to the President as the commissioning date and encouraged alignment with this timeline.

Members present contributed meaningful insights during the session, reinforcing the urgency of timely completion.

Conclusion

The monitoring exercise at Bunumbu Campus reaffirmed the collective commitment of all stakeholders to the successful completion of the ETU reconstruction project.

While commendable progress has been made across most structures, the debriefing highlighted the urgency of addressing outstanding works, particularly electrical installations, plumbing and accessibility features.

The unified call from the Vice Chancellor and Principal, the Registrar, the Ministry of Finance and the Project Coordinator underscored the importance of meeting the commissioning deadline set for December.

The engagement ended on a positive note, reflecting both appreciation for the progress achieved and determination to overcome the remaining hurdles.
